How it works

From the dock to the shop without the group text.

Problems get spotted on the dock and fixed in the shop. DockPulse connects the two so nothing gets lost between shifts.

1

Spot it

Anyone on the dock picks the boat, snaps a photo, and marks how serious it is. Takes about 30 seconds.

2

Fix it

Mechanics get a push notification immediately. They assign it, comment on it, and close it out with notes, parts, and cost.

3

Track it

Every issue, checklist, and service lands in the boat's history, so you always know what was done and when.

Do Not Send

Boat stays at the dock. Mechanics get a high priority alert.

Needs Attention

Safe to rent, but it needs a look soon.

Sendable

Cosmetic or minor. Logged so it doesn't get forgotten.

Features

Everything your crew already does, in one app.

Built for pontoons, jet skis, and fishing boats spread across multiple docks and yards.

Issue reporting

Photos, severity, and status on every problem. Comment threads keep the back and forth in one place.

Instant alerts

Push notifications for new issues, assignments, and comments. Each person picks what they want to hear about.

Pre-op and seasonal checklists

Daily pre-ops and winterization lists that several people can work on and pick back up later.

Preventive maintenance

Oil changes, lower units, impellers, and plugs tracked by engine hours or days, with due and overdue flags.

Every location

See every boat by location, move boats between docks and yards, and archive the ones you retire without losing their history.

Team to-dos

Shop and yard work organized by area, assigned to people, with due dates and status everyone can see.

Built on a working dock

Made with the mechanics and dock managers who use it.

DockPulse was built side by side with a working rental operation on the Emerald Coast. Features ship when the crew asks for them, and get tested on real boats before anyone else sees them.

Owners stay in control. You decide who can report, assign, move boats, and see each area, and your team sets up their own locations, checklists, and lists right in the app.
